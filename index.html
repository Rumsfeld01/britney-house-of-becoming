The Story

I am Britney.
I make people feel seen.

House of Becoming is not just a studio � it is a philosophy. I started draping at 15, convinced that fabric could carry weight the way words never quite could. After training in fashion design and working alongside stylists, photographers and brands across Africa and Europe, I returned to build something of my own.

My work lives at the intersection of styling and design � two crafts that, together, make a complete visual language. I style to reveal. I design to transform.

Every client, every shoot, every gown is a collaboration. We begin with who you are, and we build toward who you're becoming.

The Atelier

Where it all
happens.

Every piece begins on a sketchbook — a quick line capturing a silhouette, a mood, a feeling. From there it moves to the drape stand, where fabric begins to find its form. Pattern, cut, stitch, hand-finish. The process is slow by design.

I work with a small circle of trusted textile suppliers, prioritising natural fibres, deadstock and artisan-woven fabrics. The result is clothing that wears well, ages beautifully, and carries meaning.
